Privacy Policy for Fluvy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short: Fluvy stores everything you log only on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. We, the makers of Fluvy, never see your entries. Only for the Premium subscription Fluvy uses the service RevenueCat. It learns a random ID and your purchases, but nothing about your cycle (section 8).

2. What data Fluvy processes

Fluvy only processes data you enter yourself, for example:

  • start, length and flow of your period
  • symptoms, mood, discharge, basal temperature, weight
  • information about sex and contraception, pill intake
  • notes and diary entries, including voice input
  • your first name (optional, only for the greeting) and your settings

This is health data, a special category of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR. That is why it never leaves your device.

3. Where your data is stored

All data is stored in the app’s storage on your phone (iPhone or Android). Fluvy does not send it to anyone: not to us, not to third parties, not to a cloud.

Depending on your phone’s settings, app data may be included in your personal device backup (e.g. iCloud Backup or Google Backup). You control this backup in your phone’s settings. We have no access to it.

4. Voice input

When you dictate using your keyboard’s microphone, your operating system or keyboard app (e.g. Apple or Google) converts your speech to text under its own privacy policy. Fluvy only receives the resulting text, never an audio recording.

5. Notifications

Fluvy schedules reminders, such as for your pill or diary, as local notifications directly on your phone. No server is involved. Replies you give in a notification are also processed only on your device.

6. Backup and export

When you use “Share backup”, Fluvy creates a text containing your data and opens your phone’s share sheet. You decide where to send it, for example by email to yourself. The chosen service’s privacy policy applies.

7. App stores

Fluvy is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. When you download or buy, Apple or Google process data under their own privacy policies. If you have agreed to it on your phone, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash reports with us. These never contain your Fluvy entries.

8. Premium subscription and purchase management (RevenueCat)

Fluvy is free. Extra features come with the “Fluvy Premium” subscription. Payment is handled by Apple or Google, and we never see payment details. To know whether your subscription is active, we use the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) as a data processor.

This involves:

  • a random ID that Fluvy creates on first launch (no name, no email, no advertising ID)
  • purchase data: which plan, purchase and expiry date, trial, the receipt from Apple or Google
  • technical details such as device type, operating system, app version, language, country and the IP address of the request

Your cycle and health data is never sent to RevenueCat. The legal basis is the performance of the subscription contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The ID is also created and checked if you don’t buy anything, so Fluvy can show prices and recognise an existing subscription (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

RevenueCat also processes data in the USA. We have a data processing agreement with RevenueCat including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Purchase data is kept as long as needed for the subscription and legal retention duties. If you’d like it deleted, email us at the address above. More: RevenueCat privacy policy.

9. Links to sources

The articles in Fluvy link to studies and guidelines. When you tap a link, the website opens in your browser and its own privacy policy applies.

10. No sharing, no tracking

Fluvy contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no login. No data is sold or shared. The only exception is the purchase data for the subscription, which RevenueCat processes on our behalf (section 8).

11. Deletion

You can delete individual entries in Fluvy or remove everything at once under “More → Delete all data”. Deleting the app also deletes all stored data. Copies in your own backups are not affected.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we hold no data about you, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: view, edit, export and delete. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

13. Not a medical device

Fluvy is not a medical device and not a method of contraception. Predictions are estimates. If you have symptoms, please see your gynaecologist.

14. Changes

If Fluvy changes in a way that requires updating this policy, we will update it here and change the date above.
